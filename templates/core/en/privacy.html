What we collect

You can browse the whole site without giving us anything. The only personal data processed today is what you enter in the contact form: name, email, subject and message. It is used only to reply to you and is never sold or shared. This page will be updated when member accounts open.

Contact messages are kept while we handle your request, and for 24 months at most.

The site sets no analytics, advertising or social media cookies, hence no cookie banner. The only cookie is a technical one, exempt from consent: the form security token. Your light or dark theme choice is stored in your browser and never sent to us.
